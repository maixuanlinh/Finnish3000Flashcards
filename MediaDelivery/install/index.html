Install on your phone

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap the download button.
  2. Open the downloaded APK. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser for this preview.
  3. Open 3000 Finnish Words Preview and start studying.

The preview installs alongside the Play Store app and starts with its own progress. Existing progress and purchases stay in the Play Store app.

Study online or offline

Images and audio load as you study and are saved temporarily for reuse. In Progress → Offline study, download the full collection for offline learning. The additional download is about 429 MB and occupies storage on your phone; Wi-Fi is selected by default.
