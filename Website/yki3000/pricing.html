SIMPLE PRICING. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Before you start.

Is Premium really a one-time payment?

Yes. Premium in each of the four apps is a one-time in-app purchase. It unlocks the complete 3,000-word illustrated collection, including images, examples, sentence audio and automatic study. It is not a subscription and does not renew every month.

Do I have to pay to try the app?

No. Download the app free and try the 211 complete A-set lessons. Basic word pairs, word pronunciation, search and word reminders are available across all 3,000 words. Premium is optional.

Where do I make the payment?

Inside your chosen app, through the App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android. This website links to the free download. It does not take payments. Open Progress, then the Premium screen, to see your local price and unlock the full collection.

Does one purchase unlock all four apps?

No. Finnish iOS, Finnish Android, Swedish iOS and Swedish Android are separate apps and purchases. Each costs €11.99 once at launch in Finland, with a regular price of €24.99 after launch. Choose your language and platform above. Your app shows your local price before purchase.

Can I restore my purchase?

Use Restore Purchases in the app you bought while signed in with the same purchasing Apple Account on iOS or Google Account on Android. Restoring Premium restores purchase access; it does not restore learning history, which is stored locally on your device.

Can I use the lessons offline?

Yes. Download pictures and recordings in advance through Progress → Download for offline study (Finnish) or Offline study (Swedish). Once downloaded, your unlocked lessons can be studied offline. Automatic study runs while the app is open.
