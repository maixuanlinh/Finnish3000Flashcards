3000 Finnish Words support

Contact support@essaykingapp.com

Include your device model, Android or iOS version, app version and what happened. A screenshot can help, but remove private information first. Never send passwords or payment card details.

What can I use for free?

Basic English–Finnish cards, word pronunciation and reminders are available across all 3,000 words. The 211-word A set includes complete lessons for free. Premium is an optional one-time purchase unlocking all complete illustrated lessons, examples, translations and automatic study playback. There is no subscription.

How do I buy or restore Premium?

Open Progress and the Premium screen. The purchase button shows your local price from the App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android. To restore a previous purchase, use Restore Purchases with the same purchasing Apple Account or Google Account on that platform. Learning history stays in local app storage and is not restored by restoring Premium. iOS and Android purchases are separate.

The price is not loading

Check your internet connection and that your platform’s store is available, then retry loading the price in the app. The free cards remain available. If the issue continues, send support your app and operating system versions. A purchase cannot complete until the store supplies the product and price.

How do reminders work?

Configure word reminders from Progress and allow notifications when prompted. Check notification permissions and device settings if they do not appear. iOS Focus or Android battery restrictions can affect delivery. Reminders do not require a server account.

Why is pronunciation silent?

Check device volume and audio output. Current iOS and Android versions use ready-made recordings. An internet connection is needed for lessons that have not been downloaded; use Progress → Download for offline study to save images and audio on the phone. Automatic study runs in the foreground and pauses when the phone locks or the app leaves the foreground.

Can I move my progress?

Progress is stored locally in this app. Keep a device backup before replacing your phone or deleting the app. Artwork exports transfer artwork, prompts and memory cues; they are not a full learning-history backup. Reinstalling can remove local learning history.

Refund requests

Apple processes iOS purchases and Google Play processes Android purchases. For help with a charge, use Apple’s purchase support or Google Play’s refund support on the platform where you bought Premium.

About the app

3000 Finnish Words is an independent Finnish vocabulary learning app from Linapps. It is not affiliated with the YKI exam organiser and does not promise an exam result.
